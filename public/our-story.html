How It All Began

Our Story

2017 — The Beginning

The one where...

Their story began in 2017 with something very simple—a "Hi" and a "Hello" on Messenger. What started as a casual chat slowly grew into something more meaningful than either of them expected.

At the time, Maribel was working in the Accounting Office, while Jonathan was a service provider under CAMPCO at Franklin Baker Inc. Their relationship was very low-key in the beginning—so low-key that almost no one knew about it. If their story were a movie, this chapter would probably be called "The Secret Agents."

Their most unusual "meeting place"? Believe it or not… the comfort room. Not for the reason you might think! It was simply the safest place where they could quickly see each other and secretly exchange small gifts—usually food. A sandwich here, a snack there. It became their own little love language.

One of the funniest moments in their story happened when they planned to meet at SM Mall to watch a movie. Maribel arrived first and waited excitedly. Later, Jonathan sent her a message saying he had already arrived. Naturally, Maribel headed toward the mall entrance to meet him. But just as she reached the door, the first person she saw was not Jonathan—it was Sir Noy. And here's the twist: Sir Noy would later become one of their Ninong. Talk about an unexpected cameo in their love story! Instant panic. Worried that Sir Noy might see them together, Maribel did the only thing she could think of, she kept walking as if she hadn't seen him at all, trying to act normal while quietly whispering to herself, "Oh my God… oh my God… oh my God…" Meanwhile, Jonathan was already there… watching the entire scene unfold. And here's where it gets even better. Maribel was so focused on avoiding Sir Noy that she walked right past Jonathan… without even noticing him. Yes. She completely missed him. From Jonathan's point of view, he was probably thinking, "Wow, she must be so excited to see me!" But instead… he had to tap her just to let her know, "Uh… I'm actually right here." Little did he know, Maribel wasn't excited at all—she was simply trying to survive the moment!

But as with every real love story, their journey also faced challenges.

In 2019, Jonathan was involved in a serious vehicular accident. It was a very difficult time for both of them. He returned home to General Santos City to recover and rest, while Maribel remained in Digos. This marked the beginning of their long-distance relationship. During that time, Jonathan left his job and went through a period of sadness and uncertainty. But through it all, Maribel never left his side. She continued to encourage him, remind him of his strength, and help him believe that things would get better. Her faith and unwavering support became Jonathan's inspiration to stand up again and keep moving forward.

Then came 2020—and like everyone else in the world, they were faced with the challenges of the pandemic. Borders were closed, and for nine long months they were unable to see each other. The distance made everything even harder. Missing each other terribly, they eventually came up with a rather creative plan—with the help of Ate Kring. One day, Maribel accompanied Kring to DBM Region XII to submit a report, which conveniently gave her a valid reason to cross the border. But the real plan? She just went with ate Kring to see Jonathan in Gensan.

After nine long months apart, they were finally able to sit together and share a meal again. It felt almost like meeting for the first time all over again.

As time passed, travel restrictions slowly eased and the borders became less strict, allowing them to see each other from time to time. Jonathan continued his employment journey in General Santos, and the two of them gradually grew used to the rhythm of a long-distance relationship.

Then in 2024, something beautiful happened—Jonathan was hired at DepEd, the very same agency where Maribel was already working. From secret comfort room meetings to eventually working in the same institution, their story had truly come full circle.

From simple Messenger chats and hidden snack exchanges, to unexpected mall encounters, life's challenges, and joyful victories—their story was never about perfect timing. It was about choosing each other, again and again, through every season of life.

And now, after all those years, their journey leads them to this moment… the beginning of their next chapter together—forever.
