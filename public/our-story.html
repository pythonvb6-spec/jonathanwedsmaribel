How it all began

Our Story

Chapter One

A Simple Message, A Beautiful Story

It all started with a friend request on Facebook. Nathan added me on July 10, 2015, but I didn’t pay much attention to him at first until he finally messaged me. One simple “Hi” on Messenger became the beginning of everything. We got to know each other through a common friend. He would even tease me before, but in the end, he was the one who fell for me. Eventually, he got my phone number, and we started texting and calling almost every day. On October 5, 2015, we met in person for the first time at the Capitol. We spent time talking so we could get to know each other better. Unexpectedly, we ended up spending almost half the day together just learning more about one another.

Chapter Two

Bound by Love, Led by God

Then on October 28, 2015, I finally said yes to him. We went through so many struggles in our relationship, but those challenges became the test of how much we truly loved each other. Together, we came to know Jesus in our lives. Because of Him, we became stronger, and He became the center of our relationship. What they say is true when God leads everything, He will never let a relationship fall apart.

Chapter Three

Blessed Beyond Measure

In 2018, God blessed us with a wonderful gift in our lives. He gave us a very handsome and kind baby boy. Through him, we got to know each other even more and learned the responsibility of raising our child well. He became a blessing to our whole family because he was the first grandchild on Bel’s father’s side. Then in 2021, God blessed us again with our second child, a baby girl. It was such a great blessing for us because we were gifted with both a son and a daughter.

Forever

And Now, Forever

On June 12, 2026, in front of family and friends who have walked this journey with us, we will stand together and promise forever. This is not the end of our story — it is the most beautiful beginning of all.
