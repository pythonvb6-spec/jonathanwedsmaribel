Reminders & Notes

Arrive Early

For Immediate Family and Sponsors: Please be seated by 2:00 PM. The ceremony begins promptly at 3:00 PM.
For Guests: Please be seated by 4:00 PM. The event starts at 5:00 PM.

Note: 3:00 PM wedding ceremony is exclusive to the immediate family and sponsors only. The event will open for guests at 5:00 PM.

Unplugged Ceremony

Please be fully present during our wedding ceremony. Kindly turn off your phones and cameras, and avoid taking photos or videos during the ceremony.
We hired professional photographers and videographers to capture the moments, so just relax and enjoy with us.

Parking

Limited parking is available near the venue. Please arrange carpooling or arrive extra early.

A Little Note

While we love your little ones, our special day will be adults-only. Please enjoy a kid-free celebration with us—thank you for understanding and helping us make it a magical day for everyone!
